THE PROBLEM

A folder of photos.
Nobody has looked at it yet.

Every motor claim starts this way. A handler opens it and answers the same three questions by eye.

01What is damaged?
02How bad is it?
03What goes in the file?

THE APP

Answer all three in one browser tab

01
Outline
Pixel masks for six damage types, each with a confidence score.
02
Grade
Severity from damaged area as a share of the car.
03
Describe
A vision-language model drafts it; the masks check every claim.
04
Hand over
One summary an adjuster reads, corrects and files.

THE DESIGN THAT FOLLOWS

Weakest where claims are commonest.
So it never decides.

01No claim is approved or denied by the app.
02Surface damage routes to an adjuster by default.
03Unsupported statements are flagged, not printed.
04The corrected version is what enters the file.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Four commitments, each with a mechanism

No decision
Evidence for a person, never a figure or an outcome.
Photos stay put
No upload endpoint, no server, nothing in transit.
Limits stated
The model card carries crack at 0.44, not just the headline.
Licence kept
No CarDD imagery in the repo, the app or this deck.

Outstanding: a bias check across vehicles, lighting and photo quality — and fraud, which this model does not detect at all.
